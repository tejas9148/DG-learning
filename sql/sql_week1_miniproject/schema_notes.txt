3NF 

1. The e-commerce data contains customer, order, product, category, and order-item information that could otherwise be stored together, causing repeated data.
2. Customer-related attributes such as customer_name, email, phone, and city were separated into the customers table using customer_id as the primary key.
3. Order-related attributes were separated into the orders table and connected to customers using customer_id.
4. Product attributes such as product_name, price, and stock were separated into the products table using product_id.
5. Category information was separated into the categories table and products reference it using category_id.
6. The order_items table was used to connect orders and products while storing quantity and unit_price.
7. This separation removes repeated customer, product, and category information and reduces update, insertion, and deletion anomalies.
8. The redesign removes partial and transitive dependencies by ensuring attributes depend on the key of their respective table.
9. The resulting structure follows 3NF with primary keys identifying entities and foreign keys maintaining relationships.
